# Figure descriptions

**Figure 1.** Actual 20-vertex active sensitivity graphs for the two five-variable indicator functions. Graph A has component sizes 16 and 4 and diameters 6 and 2; graph B has component sizes 11 and 9 and diameters 4 and 4. Both sensitivity graphs have 21 edges and the same complete adjacency spectrum. The node positions illustrate adjacency; geometric distance in the drawing is not graph distance.

**Figure 2.** Four horizontally arranged control classes, from local edge and degree statistics, through coordinate-direction counts and Boolean-function complexity, to the full adjacency characteristic polynomial. Every control agrees for the pair, but their component-size and diameter profiles differ.

**Figure 3.** Three connected boxes illustrate the parity lift of a five-variable base graph to dimensions five, six and seven: the products with Q_0, Q_1 and Q_2. Every fresh parity coordinate is sensitive at every lifted input.
